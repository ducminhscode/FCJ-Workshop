### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The working environment at Amazon Web Services Vietnam is not only a modern technology office but also truly a "breeding ground" for innovation. From the very first day, I could clearly feel the enthusiasm and startup spirit within the company. Although AWS is a global technology corporation, it still maintains operational flexibility and consistently encourages interns to think boldly and take initiative.  

The professional and modern workspace, equipped with both focused working areas and open discussion spaces, made idea sharing much easier. In particular, having early access to world-leading Cloud technologies was an extremely valuable opportunity for a student like me.  

What impressed me the most was the equality and mutual respect within the workplace. The gap between Seniors, mentors and Interns was almost nonexistent during technical discussions. Every opinion was listened to and responded to constructively, truly reflecting the "Earn Trust" and "Are Right, A Lot" principles that AWS highly values.  

Overall, this is an ideal environment for anyone who wants to step outside their comfort zone and is ready to face global-scale technological challenges.

**2. Support from Mentors and Team Admin**

The guidance and support from mentors and the Admin team were truly the "key factors" that helped me quickly adapt and overcome the initial challenges during my internship at Amazon Web Services Vietnam. What impressed me most was the strong sense of "Ownership" demonstrated by the mentors in their guidance approach. Instead of simply giving step-by-step instructions, they always encouraged me to take responsibility for assigned tasks by providing direction, asking guiding questions and allowing me to explore solutions independently before reviewing and optimizing them together. This approach greatly helped me develop independent thinking and effective problem-solving skills.  

Despite their busy schedules, the mentors were always willing to spend time on 1:1 sessions to listen to my difficulties, answer questions and share valuable real-world experiences related to Cloud infrastructure deployment and system troubleshooting-knowledge that textbooks alone could never fully provide.  

I also truly appreciated the straightforward yet constructive feedback from the team because it helped me recognize areas for improvement and make noticeable progress throughout each stage of the internship.  

Looking back on the entire journey, I feel genuinely fortunate to have worked alongside such "teachers" who are not only highly skilled professionals but also deeply dedicated to nurturing the next generation.

**3. Relevance Between the Internship and My Major**

My internship at Amazon Web Services was a true "hands-on" journey that helped me strengthen and significantly expand the knowledge I learned at university. Concepts such as the OSI model, TCP/IP and Linux/Windows system administration were no longer just theoretical topics, as I had the opportunity to directly configure VPCs (Virtual Private Clouds), manage Security Groups and deploy EC2 instances on AWS. In particular, exposure to modern Cloud Computing technologies such as serverless architectures with Lambda, object storage with S3 and databases like RDS/DynamoDB helped me better understand the technological trends that global enterprises are moving toward.  

Beyond technical expertise, the internship also introduced me to professional software development workflows through Labs and small-scale projects. This allowed me to gain a deeper understanding of DevOps practices as well as Infrastructure as Code (IaC), topics that are often only introduced theoretically in academic environments.  

More importantly, the work here trained me to think analytically and solve problems logically and systematically, which aligns closely with the goals of Computer Science education: developing engineers capable of designing optimal technological solutions for real-world challenges.  

Overall, my internship at AWS was not only highly relevant to my major but also served as a perfect bridge between academic knowledge and the real-world demands of the global technology industry.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

Throughout the internship, I was able to comprehensively improve myself in terms of technical knowledge, mindset and working skills. I had the opportunity to organize my learning roadmap and define goals for pursuing valuable AWS certifications, thereby building a stronger and more practical Cloud foundation. The program was not limited to theoretical learning but also provided many hands-on experiences through practical Labs, where I directly worked with real AWS environments - from infrastructure setup and service deployment to system security optimization. In addition, the professional and multinational working environment significantly improved my soft skills, including technical presentation, professional communication and effective teamwork.  

After completing the internship, I became not only "richer" in Cloud knowledge but also more professional and confident in preparing myself for the job market.

**5. Culture and Team Spirit**

The culture at Amazon Web Services is a combination of high professional standards and a strong spirit of mutual support. I was especially impressed by the "Peculiar" mindset, where unique ideas are respected and innovation is always encouraged. Even as an Intern, I consistently received enthusiastic support from colleagues and mentors under the spirit of "Earn Trust." Technical discussions were conducted openly with very little hierarchy, creating a comfortable and approachable learning environment. This strong sense of teamwork and connection made the AWS workplace both highly professional and genuinely positive and friendly.

**6. Policies and Benefits for Interns**

The company provided me with many practical forms of support throughout my learning and development journey. I had access to a vast collection of technical documentation, blogs and international best practices from AWS, which broadened my perspective on system design and Cloud Computing. In addition, leveraging the AWS Free Tier for hands-on practice helped me strengthen my resource management skills and improve cost optimization awareness. The professional environment and opportunities to interact with top experts also gave me valuable career direction and inspiration. Overall, AWS focused on giving interns the "fishing rod" by encouraging a strong spirit of self-learning and proactive personal development.
